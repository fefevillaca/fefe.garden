about

I’m a creative consultant who turns complex ideas into popular content, which means I can be either the most imaginative person at a corporate table or the most practical one in a room full of creatives. This duality has taken me from COP15 and the G20 to music festivals like Lollapalooza and Rock The Mountain, collaborating with teams ranging from local artists to global brands.

As a marketing manager, I’ve taken on strategic roles while keeping a hands-on profile: shaping each project’s scope, leading in-house and freelance teams and getting partners, sponsors and advertisers on the same page. On the institutional side, I’ve led creative and editorial work for civil society organizations, government ministries, political leaders and party campaigns.

My goal is to create value by weaving together cultural research and project management into creative products that resonate with audiences. In the ever-evolving creative economy, I aim for culturally relevant communication, the kind that data can measure, and genuine human connections that go beyond metrics.

institutional communication

I’ve been taking on more institutional work, developing editorial strategies for events and organizations such as the G20, C40, COP 15 and Brazil’s Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change, turning complex agendas into accessible content.

  • Brasileiragem (2026): creative direction and project management of Brasileiragem, a new platform by Sleeping Giants Brasil. As creative director, I defined the platform’s concept and creative direction, from the manifesto, naming and taglines to the editorial design of all of its content. As project manager, I ran the whole operation, coordinating content production, press releases and offline actions such as the Baralho na Ponta da Língua card deck.
  • Fernando Haddad (2026): editorial direction of the campaign’s owned content and assets for Governor of São Paulo, such as video report series, Haddad’s own authored pieces and partnerships with PT. I also built the social video team that followed him throughout the campaign and set up the campaign’s editorial intelligence, turning comments on social media, press coverage and other sources into content insights.
  • Brazil’s Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change (2025 – 2026): editorial consulting for the social media of the ministry and the minister. I coordinated the video and photo teams and their deliveries for moments such as COP15, hosted by Brazil in the Pantanal, the transition between ministers and actions with partners such as Greenpeace, IBAMA and ICMBio, helping create a specific editorial line for each of them and translating complex agendas into popular content.
  • G20 (2024): coverage coordination for Bloomberg Philanthropies, in partnership with Rio de Janeiro City Hall, during the G20 in Rio. I coordinated the coverage of the plenary sessions and of the events around them, such as high-level dinners, cultural performances and visits from world leaders.
